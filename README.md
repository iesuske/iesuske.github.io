# [Glue & Chaos]

**Original UK & US Punk Vinyl Archive — 1976–1980**

★ FIRST PRESSINGS ONLY ★ NO REPRESSES ★ NO BOOTLEGS ★ NO BULLSHIT ★

---

A personal archive of original first-pressing punk vinyl from the UK and US, 1976–1980. No reissues, no repressed nostalgia — just the real labels, the real catalog numbers, the real runout groove matrix. Built one record at a time by a collector, for collectors.

**Live site:** [iesuske.github.io](https://iesuske.github.io/)

---

## What's in the crate

Sex Pistols, The Clash, The Damned, Ramones, Sham 69, Stiff Little Fingers, Cockney Rejects, The Jam, The Vibrators, The Undertones, Angelic Upstarts, The Boys, 999, Eddie & The Hot Rods, Richard Hell & The Voidoids, Dead Boys, Slaughter and the Dogs, The Adverts, Johnny Moped, The Cortinas, Chelsea, The Saints, Eater, Menace, The Lurkers, Tuff Darts, The Drones, The Skids, The Depressions, Elton Montello, The Electric Chairs, The Heartbreakers — original UK and US pressings, label and catalog details included.
